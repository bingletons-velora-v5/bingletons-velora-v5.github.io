Civil War Frontline

This is a side-scrolling history game set around Gettysburg and the wider war effort. You move through battle lines, artillery positions, a river with naval fire, and a fort assault while learning the basics of the conflict.

The art here is drawn in-game so it runs as one file. The code is set up so you can swap in realistic sprite PNGs later without changing the gameplay logic.

It includes Meade, infantry, cannon positions, ship guns, bridges, forts, flags, medics, supply crates, and a short history fact at every major area.
